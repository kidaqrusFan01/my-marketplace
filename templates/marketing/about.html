About Corazon Marketplace

Corazon Marketplace is an online marketplace connecting independent sellers with shoppers across Nigeria — everything from electronics and fashion to home essentials, books, and beauty products, all in one place.

What we do

We give independent sellers the tools to list and manage their own products, while giving shoppers a single, familiar place to browse, compare, and buy with confidence. Every order is backed by our return policy, and every purchase earns you Loyalty Coin — points you can redeem for discounts, delivery credit, or convert to airtime, data, or cash.

Our marketplace, your business

Whether you're a shopper looking for a great deal, a seller ready to reach more customers, or a business that wants to advertise with us or post job openings on our Careers page, Corazon Marketplace is built to grow with you.

Get in touch

Have a question, a partnership idea, or feedback for us? Reach out any time — we're a growing team and we'd love to hear from you.
