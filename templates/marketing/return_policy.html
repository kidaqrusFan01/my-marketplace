Return Policy

Last updated: {% now "F Y" %}

1. Return Window

Most items purchased on Corazon Marketplace can be returned within 7 days of delivery, provided they're unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging. Perishable goods, personal care items that have been opened, and items marked "Final Sale" on the product page are not eligible for return.

2. How to Start a Return

  1. Go to Your Orders and locate the item.
  2. Contact the seller (shown on the order) to request a return.
  3. Package the item securely and ship it back using the return details provided.

3. Refunds

Once a returned item is received and inspected by the seller, your refund is issued to your original payment method. If you paid partly with Loyalty Coin, the points portion is credited back to your Loyalty Coin balance rather than refunded as cash.

4. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item — these cases are prioritized and don't count against the standard 7-day window.

5. Who Pays for Return Shipping

If the return is due to a seller or fulfillment error (wrong/damaged item), return shipping is free. If you're returning an item simply because you changed your mind, return shipping is the buyer's responsibility unless the seller states otherwise.
